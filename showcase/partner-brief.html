01 / The problem

Service businesses can’t prove they’re good

Lawyers, accountants, agencies and consultants all claim the same things. Buyers trust what other clients say, not marketing.

  1. Testimonials are weak

    A one-line quote convinces nobody. A real story, on video, in the client’s own words, does.

  2. Stories are hard to get

    Owners don’t know whom to ask, what to ask, or how to film it. So they collect one or two, then stop.

  3. Search is changing

    Google and AI assistants now favour authentic, verifiable proof. Businesses without it are getting harder to find.
