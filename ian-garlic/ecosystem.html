How it fits together

One path for a client, from first contact to being the expert AI recommends. Every app below plugs into one of these six steps.

01

Attract

Workshops, ads, books and CLE bring people in. Directory listings give outbound a reason to reach out.

Workshops · CLE · Directory outbound · Black Friday bundle
02

Find the niche

The client answers a guided questionnaire and leaves with a niche, angles and who to interview.

GPS · GHL access codes
03

Plan the stories

The TackleBox picks the angles, hooks, videos and questions that matter for that client.

TackleBox · Hook finder · Storyboarder
04

Collect

An AI interviewer records short video stories with the client's customers and team.

StoryCollector · Reviews · FAQ mode
05

Certify and publish

Stories get certified and published where people and AI look: directories, client sites, YouTube.

Authentic.video · Badges · Directories · WordPress plugin · YouTube-driven sites
06

Get recommended

AI matches the client's stories to the problem a buyer describes, and sales reuses the best ones.

Context matching · YouTube Optimizer · Sales-story finder

The loop: published stories feed the TackleBox again, which tells the client what's still missing and what to record next.
