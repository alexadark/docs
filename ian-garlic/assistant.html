A day with it

Morning

Three things

You see three things, no more. Each one opens straight onto the file, the chat or the task. One line takes you back to where you stopped.

Working block

One thing at a time

You say what you're working on. The agents on that project do their part while you record, edit or decide.

Questions

One by one

Each question has a suggested answer. Say yes, change it, or skip it. Every answer is saved as you give it, so nothing is lost if you stop.

End of day

Approve and close

You go through the drafts, see what's done and what waits on others, and leave a starting point for tomorrow.

How a piece of work moves

Your tools

Email, ClickUp, Slack, Google Docs, Obsidian: what came in and what's already there.

Your assistant

Sorts it, picks the project, and keeps the thread so you don't have to.

Agents do the work

Claude or Codex drafts, prepares and checks behind the scenes.

You approve

Read it next to the chat, change a line, say yes or no.

Published or sent

Only what you approved goes out, and the result comes back to you.

One example: from a talk to a post

These follow the steps in your Garlic Press workflow. The talk and the post are made up.

Sample data

    Your mail, without the noise

    We'll set up a mail app inside your assistant, on your own account. It's the one Alexandra uses, with the same features. You can try it in the Mail tab of the screen above.

    Sorted for you

    Each email is filed by project with a one-line summary. Newsletters, promotions and cold outreach go to Filtered. Move an email and it proposes a rule for that sender.

    What needs an answer

    A To do list holds only the threads waiting on you. Your phone can alert you for those.

    The whole story

    A short summary of the thread, with the related tasks and notes next to it.

    Drafts ready

    Replies are drafted from that context, in the language of the thread. Ask for a change or write it yourself. A missing fact stays marked, and the reply can't go until it's filled in.

    You press Send

    The agents can't send email. Everything you sent is in one list and, if you turn it on, you see when it was opened.

    The rest, in its place

    Meeting bookings, travel and finance each get their own view. Archive or delete several at once, keep your contacts, use several accounts.

    What connects, and what for

    With your own accounts. It reads and drafts; sending and publishing wait for you.

    EmailFeeds the mail app: finds what needs you and drafts the reply.You send.
    ClickUpShows tasks, decisions and who you're waiting on.Your team keeps working in ClickUp.
    SlackGathers the questions for you and drafts the replies.You approve each one.
    Google DocsFinds the right document and opens it.No more searching for where it is.
    ObsidianA shared vault for notes, sources and drafts in markdown.What stays private isn't in it.

    It will run on the Mac mini at your place, so Alexandra can help and fix things remotely. You use it from your laptop or your phone. It's much simpler than the system Alexandra uses for her own work.

    What we'll set up together

    The frame

    Which projects to start with, how your day runs, the cockpit you already have, and the first result to aim for. And a question for you: what should happen with Friday?

    Garlic Press

    Your workflow as you use it today, how you want to approve, and where pieces get published.

    Ads

    How a campaign goes from idea to result, where the material lives, and what always stays your call.

    Your other processes

    Content, YouTube and the rest: list them, then pick the ones to run from one button.

    Connections

    Which accounts come first, what counts as noise in your mail, and which vault to share in Obsidian.

    Rules

    What it can do alone, when it asks, what stays private, and how we help when something breaks.

    Setup questions

    One question at a time, skip any of them. Where there's a suggested answer, a yes is enough. Start with the first block: it's enough to configure a first version. Type or dictate your answer under each one. It saves in this browser as you go.
