A day with it

Morning

Three things

You see three things, no more. Each one opens straight onto the file, the chat or the task. One line takes you back to where you stopped.

Working block

One thing at a time

You say what you're working on. The agents on that project do their part while you record, edit or decide.

Questions

One by one

Each question has a suggested answer. Say yes, change it, or skip it. Every answer is saved as you give it, so nothing is lost if you stop.

End of day

Approve and close

You go through the drafts, see what's done and what waits on others, and leave a starting point for tomorrow.

Published or sent
