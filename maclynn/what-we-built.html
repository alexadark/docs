What we built

Where each piece of the sales work stands today: what's live, what's built, what's in progress and what's paused.

How the pieces connect

The team works in Claude. Claude reaches GoHighLevel, BoldSign, Monday and Granola only through the Maclynn connector, which runs on Maclynn's own Cloudflare account. SmartMatch, PandaDoc and Mailchimp aren't connected by this work.

Sales toolkit in Claude

The maclynn-sales plugin turns a sales call into meeting notes in Tory and Alexa's template, then a proposal on Rachel's six-page template, then the agreement with the Terms and Conditions copied word for word and checked before sending. Version 0.1.31 on Nov 21.

  • Plugin on GitHub (Maclynn's account)
Live

Signature with BoldSign

Agreements go out through BoldSign: the client signs, then the salesperson who held the meeting countersigns, and GoHighLevel is updated with the signed PDF and a note. The first real client agreements went out on Nov 12 and 13.

Live

Approvals in GoHighLevel

Tory approves UK proposals and agreements and Sarah Powell approves US ones by ticking a task in GoHighLevel, with Rachel as backup. Since Nov 21, Tory's and Sarah's own agreements no longer go to Rachel, as Rachel asked on Nov 20.

Live

Agreement follow-up in Claude

Tory can ask Claude whether a client opened the agreement, get a fresh signing link to resend on WhatsApp, and revoke an agreement to send a corrected one.

Live

Payment moves the deal to Won

When the salesperson tells Claude a client has paid, the opportunity moves to Won and the matchmaker gets a single Start matchmaking task. A client who pays before signing is handled too.

Live

Sales calls from Granola

Claude reads the sales team's Granola calls through the connector, read only, to write the meeting notes.

Live

Notes and proposals on the client's record

Confirmed meeting notes and each approved proposal PDF are filed on the client's GoHighLevel contact, one note per version, so the whole team can see them.

Live

Monday leads into GoHighLevel

New website and campaign leads from Monday's Inbound Forms board reach GoHighLevel automatically since Nov 9, as a New Enquiry opportunity, with UK leads owned by Miranda. Leads marked Spam or Duplicate in Monday are tagged in GoHighLevel.

Built, not live yet

Template publisher for Rachel

Lets Rachel publish a new proposal template from Claude without help. Claude refused to add it on Nov 19; the fix is built and waiting to go live.

In progress

Alexa's sales pipeline

GoHighLevel keeps Alexa's 14-stage Sales Pipeline: a signed contract lands in Contract Signed, and only a confirmed payment moves it to Won.

In progress

Moving from Monday to GoHighLevel

A step-by-step plan for Miranda and for leaving Monday, with milestones rather than a fixed date, as Alexa asked on Nov 19.

Paused

Sales call coaching

A skill that scores a consultation against Maclynn's draft sales framework. Paused until January at Rachel's request (Nov 19); Tory's answers to the framework are kept for then.
