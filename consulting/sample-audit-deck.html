Foundation 1 of 3 · Observability and the automation-tool exit

Your automations fail in silence

  • Problem: broken automations fail silently
  • Watch every automation on every platform
  • Send every failure to one team-chat alert
  • Rebuild the live automation flows so they self-report
OutcomeEvery automation failure visible within minutes
07
Foundation 2 of 3 · Capture and the governed AI layer

Conversations vanish after they happen

  • Problem: AI used everywhere, captured nowhere
  • Move chat lines to the official business rail, no bot in sight
  • Connect every mailbox with one sign-off
  • Give the team one shared, watched AI workspace
OutcomeEvery conversation captured, clients never see a bot
08
Foundation 3 of 3 · Consolidate onto one CRM, guardrails included

One CRM reads the conversation back

Replaceable pool today: email, messaging, booking and e-sign tools
  • Problem: contacts synced unfiltered, no oversight
  • Purge the synced contacts with proof
  • Lock the sync field by field
  • Migrate to the new CRM, old and new running in parallel
OutcomeOne CRM that reads and writes the conversation timeline
09
The asset the foundation creates

Every question waits for the founder

The knowledge stops living in two heads
  • Feed the memory: doctrine, SOPs, CRM, records database, conversations
  • Let anyone ask in plain language, get a cited answer back
  • Announce it now, build it next
OutcomeThe team gets answers without asking the founder
10
Sales Director · Capture notes to proposal ⭐

Proposals are written by hand, every week

  • Problem: every proposal written by hand
  • Capture the conversation automatically, no re-typing
  • Draft the notes in the Sales Director's own format
  • Push a reviewed proposal into the existing tools
OutcomeA proposal drafted in minutes, not hours
11
Senior Consultant · Search and shortlist on the client records database

Consultants are searching by hand

  • Problem: slow search, client waits weeks
  • Make the whole records database searchable in plain English
  • Hand the consultant a shortlist to review and choose from
  • Flag a dormant record the moment it fits
OutcomeMore clients per consultant, from the database you already own

Sales Director · Lead vetting, research and sequences

Lead research eats the week

How we fix it
  • Score and check every lead automatically
  • Queue outreach sequences, revive the dormant leads
  • Audit the social-selling seats, cancel what's unused
OutcomeEvery lead vetted and sequenced, hours back every week
