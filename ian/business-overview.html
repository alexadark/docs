01 / The basics

Who Ian is, what TAMOS is

The short version you can repeat to someone else.

Ian Garlic

  • Marketer and founder of Video Case Story, an agency that films and publishes customer stories for professional service firms.
  • Host of the Garlic Marketing Show podcast, author of a book on video testimonials, and a regular speaker at workshops and conferences.
  • Has run thousands of case story interviews. His method (which questions to ask, whom to ask, how to ask) is the core know-how behind the products.

TAMOS and its mission

  • An umbrella, not a single app. TAMOS groups the agency’s services and several software products that share one goal.
  • The mission: help service businesses prove they’re trustworthy with real stories from real clients, and make those stories easy to find for buyers, Google and AI search tools.
  • Ian and Alexandra build it together. Ian leads sales and the commercial vision. Alexandra leads the technology.

04 / The logic

How it fits together

Each product handles one step. A client can enter at any step, and each step makes the next one easier to sell.

  1. The agency funds the build

    Paying clients get stories today. Every manual project shows which steps are worth turning into software.

  2. Tools open the door

    Fish in the Barrel and the Clarity Compass are cheap or free first steps. They end with a plan that StoryCollector carries out.

  3. StoryCollector is the engine

    It collects stories at scale without a film crew. Approved stories flow to TackleBox and on to publishing.

  4. Directories create demand

    A free listing brings experts in. Upgrading means collecting stories, which means StoryCollector.

  5. Publishing builds authority

    Stories on Authentic.Video, directories and YouTube-driven sites help clients show up in Google and AI answers.

  6. Partners multiply reach

    Referral partners and communities bring audiences. Ian has ties to Thrive, a large small-business network; a link-up there is an idea, not an agreement.

05 / Customers

Customer journey and channels

How a business discovers TAMOS, buys, and uses it. Most sales today come from Ian in person: workshops, talks and his network.

  1. 01Discover

    Hears about Ian

    At a workshop or talk, on the podcast, through a referral, a directory listing, or an outreach message. Paid ads are planned.

  2. 02Try

    Takes a first step

    Joins a workshop, uses Fish in the Barrel or the Clarity Compass, claims a free directory listing, or books a call.

  3. 03Buy

    Picks an offer

    A done-for-you story project, a StoryCollector plan, a low-cost monthly program, or a listing upgrade. Sales go through Ian.

  4. 04Set up

    Gets onboarded

    The team helps pick whom to interview and what to ask. Today this is done by hand; the Compass is meant to automate it.

  5. 05Collect

    Stories come in

    Customers do AI interviews through StoryCollector, or the team films them. The client approves; the team edits and titles.

  6. 06Publish

    Stories go to work

    On Authentic.Video, directories, the client’s site and YouTube. Badges link back. Then the client collects the next round.

10 / Where you fit

Where a new partner could add value

The gaps that came up again and again in recent calls and team interviews.

SALES

A second seller

Ian is the only person who sells. Someone who can run demos, follow up workshop leads and close would remove the biggest bottleneck.

OFFERS

Packaging and pricing

Many offers, few fixed prices. A clear ladder from free tool to monthly plan to done-for-you would make every channel easier.

GROWTH

Outbound and ads

Directory outreach and paid ads are designed but not proven. Someone to run, measure and tune them, within the rules for email and reviews.

OPERATIONS

Running the delivery line

Footage handoffs, title approvals and client updates need one clear owner. An operator who runs the process would speed up every project.

DATA

Measurement and reporting

Nobody owns results today. Showing clients which stories brought views, clicks and leads would prove the whole idea and help renewals.

CHANNELS

Partnerships and verticals

Networks of agencies, assistants or associations could each bring many businesses. A partner with access to one vertical could launch its directory.
