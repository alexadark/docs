Four steps. You can do them yourself with our tools, or let our team do them for you.

04 / Questions

Common questions

Do I need to be on the interview?

No. You set the direction and share a link. The AI interviewer runs the conversation; you review the result.

Will my clients find it awkward to talk to an AI?

The interviewer asks natural follow-up questions, like a good journalist. Clients can stop and come back if they get interrupted.

Does the AI make up reviews?

No. Written reviews are built from your client’s exact words, and they approve them before anything is shared.

Is anything posted automatically?

No. Approving, sending and publishing are separate steps, and only your client can post a review to Google.

What kind of businesses is this for?

Service businesses that sell on trust: law and tax firms, agencies, consultants, clinics, coaches, and similar.
